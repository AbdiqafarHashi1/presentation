The Opportunity

Right now, Liibaan Libaax is one book with an early audience. The bigger opportunity is to grow it into a media brand. Content brings in new families, and the website converts that attention into book, bundle, and digital sales.

How the System Works

Book / Story IP → Content → Audience → Website → Sales → Growth

Each cycle improves visibility, trust, and conversion. Over time, this creates a stronger brand and more efficient growth than relying on one-off campaigns.
